About USIP

Sport to serve and protect.

Founded in Paris on 23 May 1990, the International Police Sports Union unites police sports federations from 71 countries and stages the World Police Championships and the USIP World Police Games.

The International Police Sports Union was founded in Paris on 23 May 1990, on the initiative of police sports activists from Spain and France. Forty-three countries signed as founding members.

USIP describes itself as the official world police sport union, organising World Police Championships in athletics, judo, cycling, shooting, futsal and related disciplines, and staging the USIP World Police Games — first held in Bogotá in 2015.

Today the Union counts 71 member countries across 4 continental regions, with its head office in Kuwait and its Secretariat at Castle Staverden in the Netherlands.

Competition

Championships and social sport

World Police Championships are held every year according to the calendar of events — cross-country, marathon, triathlon, judo, cycling, shooting, futsal and other police-related sports — deepening cultural understanding and professional exchange between officers worldwide.

Beyond competition, the Union supports social sport projects through its "Hold my Hand" foundation, carrying the motto — sport to serve and protect — into communities.

Governance

Congress and committees

The Congress is the highest authority of USIP. It convenes every two years, and every member nation holds an equal vote regardless of size. The Executive Committee is elected for four-year terms.

A Technical Committee, chaired by the Secretary General, writes the competition rules and supervises the World Police Championships, with members drawn from Bahrain, the UAE, Brazil, Kuwait, Spain, Saudi Arabia and Romania.

Our history

From Paris 1990 to the World Police Games

Three decades of police sport — a union founded by 43 countries that now spans four continental regions.

  1. 1990

    Founded in Paris

    The International Police Sports Union was founded in Paris on 23 May 1990, on the initiative of police sports activists from Spain and France. Forty-three countries signed as founding members, from Argentina and China to Kuwait, Morocco and the United Kingdom.

    43founding countries

  2. 1990s–2000s

    World Police Championships

    The Union built an annual calendar of World Police Championships — cross-country, marathon, triathlon, judo, cycling, shooting, futsal and related police sports — with the Congress convening as the highest authority and every member nation holding an equal vote.

  3. 2015

    The first World Police Games

    Bogotá staged the inaugural USIP World Police Games from 26 September to 3 October 2015. More than 500 athletes from 30 countries competed in shooting, swimming, athletics, taekwondo and cycling, and the National Police of Colombia topped the first edition.

    500+athletes in Bogotá

  4. 2017–2019

    Abu Dhabi and Milan

    The Games grew fast: Abu Dhabi 2017 drew more than 800 police athletes from 51 nations across four disciplines, and Milan-Lombardia 2019 staged seven disciplines across seven venues in the region, from practical shooting to the 10K.

    51nations in Abu Dhabi

  5. 2020–2023

    Through the pandemic

    The 16th Congress and the planned fourth Games were postponed by the pandemic. USIP returned to competition in November 2023 with the 1st World Police Futsal World Cup in Kuwait, played under FIFA and USIP regulations.

  6. 2024–present

    The road to Santo Domingo

    In February 2024 General Gonzalo Londoño was appointed Secretary General, and in January 2025 Santo Domingo was announced as host of the Fourth USIP World Police Games 2026 — carrying the Games to their fourth continent-spanning edition.
