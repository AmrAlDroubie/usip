Sport to serve and protect.

Uniting police athletes worldwide since 1990. The International Police Sports Union joins police federations from 71 countries in World Police Championships and the USIP World Police Games.

Athletics

Cross-country, road running and the marathon. Running events anchor the USIP calendar, from the annual World Police Championships to the 10K at the World Police Games.

Explore→

Judo

A cornerstone of police sport. USIP holds a memorandum of understanding with the International Judo Federation, and judo has featured at the Games since Abu Dhabi 2017.

Explore→

Swimming

Pool racing has been contested at every edition of the USIP World Police Games, from Bogotá 2015 through Abu Dhabi 2017 to Milan-Lombardia 2019.

Explore→

Shooting

Service pistol, practical shooting and skeet & trap — including the World Police Service Pistol Shooting Championships staged in Hungary in 2015 and in Guangdong, China in 2018.

Explore→

Futsal

The 1st USIP World Police Futsal World Cup was staged in Kuwait in November 2023 — played under FIFA and USIP regulations, with a field capped at 32 national police teams.

USIP

The International Police Sports Union (USIP) was founded in Paris on 23 May 1990 on the initiative of police sports activists from Spain and France. Today it unites police sports federations across four continental regions, organises World Police Championships and stages the USIP World Police Games.
